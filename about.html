About me / 01

More than a transcript.

The short version: I'm a final-year computer science student who cares about the space where thoughtful design and solid engineering meet. The longer version has a few detours, a camera, and an unreasonable number of notebooks.

Based in Lagos, Nigeria B.Sc. Computer Science Class of 2027

The short story

I got here through small obsessions.

My first encounter with programming was an attempt to make a game run on a laptop that was never designed to run it. It failed spectacularly. Then I spent a weekend learning why, and that was the beginning of the end.

Six years later, I've traded game mods for design systems. What stayed with me is the process: understand the constraint, sketch the simplest path, build it, then smooth every rough edge you can find. I care as much about the empty state of a task list as the headline on the homepage.

At university, I focus on web engineering and human-computer interaction. Outside it, I photograph quiet corners of Lagos, run slowly, and lose to the same chess opening every time.

Career aspirations

Where I'm headed.

I don't have the next ten years mapped out. I do know the kind of work that makes me lose track of time — and the problems I want to be useful for.

  1. Join a product team that sweats the details

    Start as a front-end engineering intern, then grow into a junior engineer owning real surfaces end to end.

  2. Study how people actually use the web

    Pursue an M.Sc. in Human-Computer Interaction, with a focus on low-bandwidth, multilingual interfaces.

  3. Build tools for African classrooms

    Design local-first learning and planning tools that work on modest devices and intermittent connections.

  4. Make the door wider for the next person

    Contribute to open source, mentor younger students, and share what I learn in public — including the mistakes.
