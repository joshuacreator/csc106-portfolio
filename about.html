About me / 01

More than a transcript.

I'm a Software Engineering student at Miva Open University and a mobile developer in Abuja. I've shipped finance and education apps, and I'm now grounding that hands-on work in a formal engineering education.

Based in Abuja, Nigeria B.Sc. Software Engineering Year 1 · Class of 2030

The short story

I got here through small obsessions.

I started by taking computers apart before I understood what software engineering was. Troubleshooting, reinstalling, and asking too many questions eventually turned into an interest in building things that work reliably for other people.

That interest became a craft. I completed a National Innovation Diploma in Computer Science in 2024, then moved into contract roles building Flutter apps across finance and education. I've launched products on the App Store and Google Play, built secure payment and verification flows, and introduced automated testing to a team that had none.

I'm now studying Software Engineering at Miva Open University, where I'm grounding that practical experience in the fundamentals. Outside work and study, I photograph streets, go sightseeing, and travel when I can.

Career aspirations

Where I'm headed.

I'm early in my career, and deliberately so. I know the kind of work I want to get better at and the problems I want to be useful for.

  1. Grow into a dependable product engineer

    Keep shipping mobile products end to end, from planning through release, with the quality bar rising each time.

  2. Go deeper on the fundamentals

    Use my degree to strengthen the database, architecture, and testing knowledge behind the screens.

  3. Build tools for African classrooms

    Take what I learned shipping education products and make learning tools that work on modest devices and unreliable connections.

  4. Make the door wider for the next person

    Keep contributing to open source, share what I learn, and mentor the next developer where I can.
